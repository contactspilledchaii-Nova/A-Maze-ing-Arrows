Privacy Policy for A-Maze-ing Arrows

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Developer: Spilled Chai

Spilled Chai built the A-Maze-ing Arrows mobile game as an ad-supported application with optional In-App Purchases. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our game, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or player profile:

  • Google Play Games Services: Used to authenticate your player account, maintain your progress across devices, and link your player profile.
  • LootLocker: Used to manage global leaderboards, store your chosen in-game display name, and record leaderboard scores.
  • Unity Ads: Used to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements. Unity Ads may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process optional In-App Purchases (such as ad removal). Payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play and are never accessed or stored by Spilled Chai.

2. Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Ads)
  • LootLocker

3. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Your leaderboard display name and score are stored on LootLocker servers as long as the game's online services remain active. If you wish to request deletion of your leaderboard profile or associated player data, please contact us at contact.spilledchai@gmail.com with your in-game display name.

4. Children's Privacy

A-Maze-ing Arrows is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: contact.spilledchai@gmail.com.
